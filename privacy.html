Assistant: privacy policy

Assistant is a personal app used only by its developer, Daniel Swingle, with his own Google account. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

With his consent, Assistant accesses his Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets and Google Contacts data, to read and manage them when he asks it to.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to carry out his requests.
  • The sign-in token is stored on his own computer and is not shared.
  • Data is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone else.

Assistant's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
